About CCTC

Consolatrix College of Toledo City Inc. (CCTC) is a private, Catholic educational institution in Toledo City, Cebu, founded and managed by the Augustinian Recollect Sisters. Established in 1961 as Consolatrix Academy, it was initially a response to the community's need for a Catholic high school. Over time, it expanded its offerings to include various undergraduate degree programs, leading to its evolution into a college. CCTC is officially recognized by both the Department of Education (DepEd) and the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), ensuring its full compliance with national educational standards. Its commitment to quality is further demonstrated by the Basic Education program's Level III accreditation from the Philippine Accrediting Association of Schools, Colleges and Universities (PAASCU), a testament to its strong academic foundation and modern educational practices.

Purpose of AEM

The Academic Event Management (AEM) system was designed to make it easier for both students and faculty to organize, monitor, and participate in events such as seminars, workshops, and conferences. It ensures a smoother process for managing schedules, registrations, and updates — enhancing academic engagement and collaboration.
